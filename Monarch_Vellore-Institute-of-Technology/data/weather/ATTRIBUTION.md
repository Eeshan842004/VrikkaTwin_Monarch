# Weather data attribution

Hourly weather for 1 March to 30 June of 2024 (training season), 2025 and 2026 (held-out test seasons) from the [Open-Meteo Historical Weather API](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/historical-weather-api), based on ERA5 reanalysis (Copernicus Climate Change Service). Licence: CC BY 4.0. Weather data by Open-Meteo.com. ERA5 is a ~25 km reanalysis: peaks are smoother (lower) than station readings and there is no urban heat island.
